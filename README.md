# Barokah Wealth

> Know where your wealth stands today.

This repository is the internal product workspace for Barokah Wealth — a wealth management companion for Muslims in Indonesia built around Islamic financial principles.

It documents the full product lifecycle: from discovery and strategy through design, engineering, and launch.
