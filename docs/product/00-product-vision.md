# 00 — Product Vision

**Barokah Wealth**
> Know where your wealth stands today.

---

## The Problem

Most Muslims in Indonesia manage their wealth across multiple asset types — cash savings and gold — but have no single place to see it all together.

This creates a real religious gap: to fulfill Zakat obligations correctly, a Muslim must know whether their total net wealth has exceeded the **Nisab threshold** and whether it has stayed above that threshold for a full lunar year (**Haul**). Without a clear picture of total wealth, this calculation is guesswork.

---

## The User

**Primary persona:** A Muslim man in his late 20s to mid-30s. Married with a young family. Works full-time and manages a small side business alongside his household finances. He is digitally comfortable but not financially sophisticated — he knows Zakat is obligatory but has never tracked it properly because the process feels complicated.

He holds wealth in multiple forms:
- Cash (salary, savings, business capital)
- Gold (physical or digital)

He thinks in **Rupiah (IDR)** but gold fluctuates in grams and IDR-linked prices, adding cognitive load.

---

## The Core Insight

The obstacle isn't awareness — he knows Zakat exists. The obstacle is **visibility**. He cannot easily answer the question:

> *"Is my total wealth above Nisab right now, and has it been for the past year?"*

Barokah Wealth answers that question, automatically, every day.

---

## The Solution

A wealth tracking companion that:

1. **Aggregates total net wealth** across cash and gold — displayed in IDR. Users can create multiple named cash pockets to organize personal, business, or any other IDR holdings.
2. **Converts gold** to IDR automatically using real-time Galeri24 gold buyback price, verifiable at galeri24.co.id
3. **Compares total wealth against the current Nisab threshold** (based on 85g gold equivalent)
4. **Tracks the Haul** — starts a 12-month lunar calendar countdown the moment the user first exceeds Nisab
5. **Notifies the user** when Haul is complete and Zakat becomes due
6. **Calculates the Zakat amount** owed (2.5% of total qualifying wealth)

---

## What It Is Not

- Not primarily a budgeting app — expense tracking is optional, not required. Users can choose to log every transaction for precision, or simply update their balance periodically. Either way, every income entry triggers a live Nisab check so the user always knows how far they are from obligation.
- Not a banking integration (manual entry, user-controlled)
- Not a fatwa engine (no religious rulings beyond standard Zakat on wealth)

---

## Vision Statement

To be the simplest, most trusted tool for Indonesian Muslims to understand their wealth position and fulfill their Zakat obligation with confidence — without needing an accountant or an Islamic finance degree.

Future versions will expand to other Muslim-majority markets — Malaysia, Singapore, and beyond — with multi-currency support and localized gold price sources.

---

## Success Looks Like

- A user opens the app and within 2 minutes knows exactly whether they owe Zakat
- A user receives a Haul completion notification and can pay Zakat with confidence in the amount
- A user tracks their gold value in IDR without manually checking gold prices
